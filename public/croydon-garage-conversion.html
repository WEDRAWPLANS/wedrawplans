Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission to convert my garage in Croydon?

Not always. A garage conversion may be permitted development, but site-specific conditions, previous permissions and external changes matter. A Lawful Development Certificate can confirm that a particular proposed scheme is lawful.

Does a garage conversion need Building Regulations approval?

Typically yes. Conversion to habitable accommodation commonly involves requirements for structure, insulation, damp control, ventilation, fire safety and electrical work. The planning certificate alone does not establish Building Regulations compliance.

Can I convert my garage into a bedroom, office or living room?

Often a garage can be adapted into additional accommodation within the home, subject to planning restrictions, suitable design and the existing structure. Whether your proposal is suitable requires an individual assessment.

Can WEDRAWPLANS provide a fixed-fee drawing package?

Yes. We agree a defined scope covering relevant planning and Building Regulations drawing services and structural coordination where included. Council, Building Control and other statutory fees are separate.
